Running Science

Training paces explained

Each zone targets a different physiological adaptation. Your running plan uses all five.

Easy Pace

Foundation Building

Warm-ups, cool-downs, and long runs. Conversational. Builds aerobic base.

65–79% HRmax
Marathon Pace

Race Conditioning

Sustained effort at goal marathon intensity. Teaches race pace efficiency.

80–90% HRmax
Threshold Pace

Lactate Threshold

Comfortably hard running or cruise intervals. Raises the sustainable ceiling.

88–92% HRmax
Interval Pace

VO2max Development

Hard 3–5 min efforts. 800m–1000m repeats. Maximizes VO2max.

98–100% HRmax
Rep Pace

Speed & Economy

Short fast efforts (200–400m) with full recovery. Improves running economy.

~1500m race pace · Full recovery

FAQ

Common questions

Built by a Clinical Exercise Physiologist — not a personal trainer or software developer. Every recommendation is grounded in current peer-reviewed exercise science. Most apps generate generic plans. This generates plans the way a clinical exercise physiologist prescribes them.
Your maximum heart rate is estimated from your age using a validated formula used in clinical exercise physiology. Moderate intensity = 64–76% of max HR. Vigorous = 77–95% of max HR. All age-predicted formulas carry a margin of error of roughly ±10–12 bpm. A directly measured max HR from a supervised maximal exercise test is always more accurate.
